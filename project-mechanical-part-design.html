Mechanical Part Design

A sample CAD project focused on turning a simple mechanical idea into a clear, manufacturable component.

Project Overview

This learning project explores the design of a compact mounting bracket for a small mechanical assembly. The work covers concept sketches, dimension planning, 3D modeling, and a basic design review.

What I Practiced

  • Created a dimensioned concept from a functional requirement.
  • Built a parametric 3D model with basic assembly relationships.
  • Reviewed hole placement, material use, and ease of manufacture.

Why It Inspires Me

AMZ Racing from ETH Zurich inspires me a lot through its advanced vehicle design and engineering teamwork. This page is an inspiration-based future project idea, not a project completed with AMZ Racing. In the future, I will try to make a similar mechanical design project and develop my CAD skills.
