Workshop Process Study

A sample manufacturing study that maps workshop activities from material preparation to final inspection.

Project Overview

This study follows a small-batch workshop process and identifies the steps that affect time, quality, and operator safety. It is designed as a practical exercise in observing manufacturing systems.

Study Focus

  • Mapped the sequence of cutting, machining, deburring, and inspection.
  • Recorded possible waiting points and repeated handling steps.
  • Suggested clearer workstation organization and quality checkpoints.

Why It Inspires Me

Team Bath Racing inspires me a lot through its practical vehicle development and workshop-based engineering. This is an inspiration-based future project idea, not work completed with Team Bath Racing. In the future, I will try to make a similar workshop process study and learn how to improve production flow.
