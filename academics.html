Academic Snapshot

The curriculum is designed to build a foundation in engineering science, machine design, production systems, advanced manufacturing, and research capability.

Study Priorities

Engineering mathematics, thermodynamics, mechanics, materials, manufacturing technology, CAD/CAM, computer-based analysis, and quality engineering are key academic priorities.

Course Flow

The program moves through fundamental science, applied mechanics, technical drawing, manufacturing processes, machine systems, simulation, and graduation practice.

Learning Output

My target is to translate theory into practical modeling, design decisions, manufacturing awareness, and professional reporting.

Current Academic Direction

01
Language & Technical Communication

Improve Chinese technical communication and professional confidence.

02
Mechanical Design & Manufacturing

Strengthen design generation, part development, and production understanding.

03
Automation & Simulation

Connect manufacturing systems with control, performance analysis, and process intelligence.

04
IELTS & International Preparation

Prepare for a future IELTS exam and build the English communication needed for internships, summer schools, and research opportunities in China and abroad.
