Smart Systems Concept

A sample automation concept for monitoring machine conditions and supporting faster technical decisions.

Project Overview

This concept combines sensors, a controller, and a simple dashboard to track temperature, vibration, and operating status. The goal is to understand how basic automation can support preventive maintenance.

Concept Features

  • Outlined sensor inputs for temperature and vibration monitoring.
  • Designed a basic PLC logic flow for normal and warning states.
  • Planned a dashboard view for status updates and maintenance notes.

Why It Inspires Me

Formula Student Team Delft inspires me a lot through its work on electric and autonomous vehicle systems. This is an inspiration-based future project idea, not work completed with Team Delft. In the future, I will try to make a similar smart monitoring project and keep learning about sensors, PLCs, and automation.
